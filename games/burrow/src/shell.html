How to dig

A / D · ← →
Walk. One-tile ledges step up on their own.
Space / W / ↑
Jump, swim up, or climb ladders. S / ↓ climbs down.
Hold click
Use the selected item on the tile under the cursor: dig with the Pick, place blocks, eat food, open doors.
Right click / F
Swing your sword. It works whatever you are holding.
1 to 9 · wheel
Choose an item.
C
Open crafting. Most recipes need a workbench close by.
P / Esc
Pause.
Touch
Tap the world to use the selected item; pads move, jump and attack.
Gamepad
Left stick moves, A jumps, right stick aims, RT uses, RB attacks, Y crafts, d-pad changes item, Start pauses. Controls are fixed for now.

Stone and ore need a good enough Pick. Falling hurts, lava kills, and the dark is full of things. Torches keep you safe to see. Water and lava flow, and when they meet they leave obsidian.
