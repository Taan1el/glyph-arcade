How to command

Click / Right-click
Move to ground, or attack the enemy under the cursor.
A + Click
Attack-move toward a spot, fighting the nearest foe along the way.
Arrow keys / WASD
Move your hero directly.
Q / W / E / R · 1–4
Cast abilities toward the cursor. R (ultimate) unlocks at level 6.
B · S · Tab
Recall to base (4s) · Open base shop · Scoreboard.
Touch
Tap to move or attack; tap an ability button on the bar or drag from a button to aim.
Gamepad
Left stick moves, A attacks nearest foe, X/Y/B/RB cast Q/W/E/R, LB recalls, Select opens shop, Start pauses.
