How to play Lockwork

W A S D · Arrows
Step 1 tile, or walk into crates [#] and conduits [+][-][|] to push them.
Space · E · Enter
Rotate an adjacent mirror [/][\] or splitter [T], or wait 1 tick in place.
Z · Backspace
Undo your last step (unlimited).
R · H · L
Reset vault · Show Hint · Vault Select menu.
P · Esc
Pause.
Touch
Tap adjacent floor/mechanisms directly on the vault, swipe, or use the bottom pads.
Gamepad
Left stick or D-pad steps, A rotates/waits, B undoes, Y resets, Select opens vaults, Start pauses.

Every step advances the clock by 1 tick: Pulse Coils (P) fire on even ticks, Delay Capacitors [1][2] release beams 1 or 2 ticks later, and Inverters [!] fire only when unlit.
