Tactical manual

  
How to play Inkfire

  

    
W / S · ↑ ↓
Move forward / backward.

    
A / D
Strafe left / right.

    
Q / E · ← → · Mouse
Turn left / right (click view for mouse look).

    
Space / F / Click
Fire current weapon.

    
1 / 2 / 3 / 4
Switch weapons: Pistol, Shotgun, Chaingun, Rocket Launcher.

    
P / Esc
Pause game. M resizes the radar map.

    
Gamepad
Left stick moves, right stick turns, RT or A fires, X uses, LB / RB change weapon, Start pauses. Controls are fixed for now and cannot be remapped.
