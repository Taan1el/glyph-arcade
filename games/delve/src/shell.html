Every stone, monster and coin here is drawn in coloured letters. Five floors down, a lich waits in the cold. Walk into a monster to strike it. Turns pass only when you act.

Field notes
How to delve

  

    
W / S · ↑ ↓
Step forward / back. Walking into a monster attacks it; into a door opens it.

    
A / D
Sidestep left / right.

    
Q / E · ← →
Turn. Turning is free; every other action is one turn.

    
1 / 2
Drink a healing potion / read a scroll of mapping.

    
Enter
Descend when standing on the stairs (>).

    
Space
Wait one turn. M resizes the map. Esc opens this page.

    
Gamepad
Stick or d-pad steps, LB / RB turn, A waits, X potion, Y scroll, B descends, Start opens this page. Controls are fixed for now and cannot be remapped.
