How to play Ricochet

W A S D · Arrows
Move on your half of the court, and steer throw aim / spin.
J / Enter / Click
Hold to charge throw, release to fire (hold too long and you fumble!). Tap at impact to parry.
K / E / Right-click
Curve-spin throw that bends in mid-air.
Space / Shift
Dash to intercept fast discs.
P / Esc
Pause.
Touch
Left pad moves and aims; Throw, Spin, and Dash buttons on the right.
Gamepad
Left stick moves/aims, A throws/parries, X spins, B or RB dashes, Start pauses.
