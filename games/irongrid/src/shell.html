Controls & tactical field guide

Enemies lock relative target arrows (^ V < >) and rings ((X)) on Turn Start. Pushing an enemy shifts its locked attack with it!

Click / Tap
Click a blue square to move rig; select a card (1-4) and click target. On touch, tap target once to preview push, tap again to fire.
WASD / Arrows
Move tactical cursor across the 8×8 grid.
Space / Enter
Confirm cursor tile (select rig, move rig, or fire selected card).
1 / 2 / 3 / 4
Select Command Card 1–4 (also selects card reward 1–3 after each battle).
Tab / Q
Cycle through standing steam-walkers.
Z / Backspace
Undo rig movement before playing a Command Card (Easy grants 1 Card Rewind/battle).
E
End Player Turn and trigger Resolution Phase.
Knockback
Bumping mountain/unit deals +1 collision damage to both. Water (~) and chasm kill ground foes instantly.
Fissures (%)
Stand on an emerging fissure at turn end to block the spawn for 1 self-damage.
Gamepad
Stick/D-pad moves cursor, A confirms, B undo/cancel, LB/RB cycle rigs, X/Y cycle cards, Start end turn / pause.
